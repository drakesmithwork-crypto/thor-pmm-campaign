Historical Fusus integration findings

Patterns observed

  • Agencies frame operations around a unified common operating picture.
  • Most deployments are phased and preserve existing camera/tool investments.
  • Distributed operations can work without a single dedicated RTCC room.
  • Alert-driven workflows reduce hand-off friction in time-sensitive incidents.

PMM implications

  • Lead with response coordination and human judgment, not autonomous framing.
  • Position rollout as governance-gated modernization, not abrupt transformation.
  • Anchor value claims to measurable workflow outcomes.
  • Proactively address surveillance and transparency concerns.

Community advisory board foundation

The advisory model is designed to gather early, structured input from students, parents, educators, and district stakeholders before campaign finalization.

Starter charter scope

  • Acceptable use and non-use boundary review
  • Privacy and data-governance guardrail feedback
  • Trust and language testing for public communications
  • Transparency and oversight recommendations

Membership model and operations

  • 12-15 seats across students, parents, educators, and administrators
  • Biweekly startup cadence with published agendas and minutes
  • Structured recommendation format with majority/minority notes
  • 30-day launch plan: charter, onboarding, first recommendation memo

Rough end-to-end operational flow (alert to dispatch)

  1. Signal trigger: incident signal generated by approved source.
  2. Alert correlation: platform enriches incident with available context.
  3. Human validation: authorized operator confirms urgency and legitimacy.
  4. Escalation gate: policy-qualified decision to launch support or monitor.
  5. Live operations: responders receive situational picture and updates.
  6. Dispatch hand-off: validated incident package informs assignment decisions.
  7. Closure workflow: stabilization, case closure, audit and retention controls.
Stage Human owner Technical action Policy checkpoint
Signal trigger School staff or systems Incident alert created Trigger category valid
Review and escalation GSOC or command operator Context verification and launch command Human review and SOP criteria met
Dispatch hand-off Liaison and dispatcher Incident package transfer Sharing rules followed
Case closure Incident command and district safety lead Case close and timeline finalization Retention and audit policy applied
