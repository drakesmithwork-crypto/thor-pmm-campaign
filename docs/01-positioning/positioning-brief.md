# Thor Positioning Brief

## Document Purpose
Define how Thor should be framed for school safety decision-makers, parents/educators, and public safety partners so the value proposition is clear, credible, and trust-forward.

## Product Category Statement
Thor is a school emergency response support capability: a Fusus-integrated aerial system that can provide real-time situational awareness to authorized responders during verified critical incidents.

## Core Problem Statement
During rapidly evolving school emergencies, the first minutes are often marked by fragmented information, delayed visibility, and coordination gaps between school personnel, GSOC operators, and dispatch. These gaps can increase risk and slow protective action.

## Positioning Statement
For school districts and public safety partners responsible for protecting students and staff, Thor is an emergency situational awareness system that helps authorized responders assess active threats faster and coordinate response with greater clarity. Unlike static-only or single-source alerting approaches, Thor combines aerial visibility, integrated camera context, and a human-governed operational workflow through Fusus.

## Audience-Specific Value Messaging

### District Leaders and School Boards
- Improve emergency decision quality with faster, clearer incident context.
- Strengthen district readiness with a defined incident workflow and accountability checkpoints.
- Reduce operational uncertainty by connecting alerts, visual confirmation, and dispatch coordination in one ecosystem.
- Support duty-of-care obligations with auditable governance and clear operational boundaries.

### Parents and Educators
- Designed for critical incidents, not routine student monitoring.
- Operates under explicit privacy limits, access controls, and policy guardrails.
- Keeps human decision-makers in control of activation, escalation, and response actions.
- Prioritizes student and staff safety while preserving trust and transparency.

### Public Safety Partners
- Accelerate incident assessment with shared visual context.
- Improve interagency coordination through standardized hand-offs from school to GSOC to dispatch.
- Increase responder preparedness before arrival with live situational data.
- Improve after-action review quality through structured incident records.

## Why Thor vs Existing Approaches

### Status Quo Limitations
- Fixed cameras may not provide complete or timely incident perspective.
- Panic alerts can signal urgency but may lack sufficient context for optimal response.
- Communication chains can introduce delays and interpretation gaps.

### Thor Differentiation
- Multi-asset fusion: Sentry alerts, camera context, and drone perspective in one operational view.
- Human-governed flow: launch and dispatch decisions remain with authorized operators.
- Integrated workflow: supports faster transition from alert to informed response.
- Policy-ready framing: built with explicit governance, accountability, and trust safeguards.

## Messaging Pillars
1. Faster trusted visibility in critical moments.
2. Human control and accountable operations.
3. Safety outcomes with clear privacy boundaries.
4. Integrated school-to-public-safety coordination.

## Elevator Pitch
Thor helps schools and responders gain rapid, reliable situational awareness during verified critical incidents so they can make better-informed decisions in the moments that matter most, while operating within clear privacy and governance limits.

## Language Guidance

### Recommended Language
- Emergency response support
- Situational awareness
- Human-governed activation
- Privacy-by-policy operations
- Authorized access only

### Avoid Language
- Autonomous surveillance
- Always watching
- AI tracking students
- Full-campus monitoring
- Automated policing

## Proof Points Needed from Engineering/Product
- Activation trigger conditions and authorization logic.
- Typical deployment timeline from verified alert to aerial visibility.
- Integration scope across Fusus, cameras, and dispatch workflows.
- Flight and operational constraints (duration, environment limits, fallback modes).
- Audit logging and role-based access mechanics.

## Potential Objections and Response Direction
- "This sounds like surveillance." -> Clarify purpose limitation and non-routine operation.
- "Who sees student footage?" -> Define role-based access, approvals, and auditability.
- "Will this replace human judgment?" -> Emphasize human-in-the-loop controls.
- "Is this realistic operationally?" -> Anchor claims to validated SW/ME capabilities.

## Claim Discipline Rules
- Do not claim fully autonomous decision-making.
- Do not imply always-on monitoring.
- Do not promise outcomes unsupported by product telemetry or pilot data.
- Do not position Thor as a substitute for school safety staff or law enforcement protocols.
