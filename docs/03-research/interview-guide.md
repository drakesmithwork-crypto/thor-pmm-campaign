# Advisory Board Interview and Discussion Guide

## Moderator Goals
- Build psychological safety and encourage honest feedback.
- Understand both practical utility perceptions and trust concerns.
- Convert qualitative feedback into actionable PMM and policy direction.

## Opening Script (Template)
"Thank you for participating. We are evaluating how to communicate and govern a school emergency response technology concept. This session is for feedback, not a sales presentation. We want honest input on what would build trust, what would cause concern, and what information you would need before supporting any rollout."

## Warm-Up Questions (All Groups)
1. What does "school safety" mean to you in practice?
2. What makes you trust or distrust a new safety technology?
3. What is one thing schools often get wrong when communicating safety initiatives?

## Concept Reaction Questions (All Groups)
1. What is your first reaction to this concept?
2. Which part feels most useful during a critical incident?
3. Which part feels most concerning or unclear?
4. What assumptions would you make if you heard this described publicly?

## Trust and Privacy Questions
1. What boundaries must be in place for this to be acceptable?
2. Who should be able to activate the system, and under what conditions?
3. Who should have access to incident footage and logs?
4. What retention timeframe feels appropriate and why?
5. What would make this feel like surveillance instead of safety support?

## Human Control and Accountability Questions
1. Where should human review be mandatory?
2. What decisions should never be automated?
3. What oversight structure would increase confidence?
4. What public transparency commitments are essential?

## Role-Specific Deep-Dive Questions

### Students
- Would this make you feel safer, less safe, or both depending on context?
- What language in student communications would feel respectful and clear?
- What misuse concerns would you want addressed first?

### Parents/Guardians
- What would you want to see in a parent FAQ before rollout?
- What are your top 3 privacy concerns?
- What would make you support or oppose district adoption?

### Educators
- How should this align with existing lockdown and emergency protocols?
- What training would staff need to trust operations?
- What communication should teachers receive during an active incident?

### Administrators/Safety Leads
- What governance artifacts are required before pilot consideration?
- What adoption criteria would your board likely require?
- What operational evidence would justify budget allocation?

## Messaging Test Exercise
Present three draft message options and ask:
1. Which statement is clearest?
2. Which statement feels most trustworthy?
3. Which phrase should be removed to avoid fear or confusion?

## Closing Questions
1. What is the single most important condition for your support?
2. What unanswered question is most important before any deployment?
3. What should we communicate first to your community?

## Moderator Notes and Coding Tags
Tag responses as one or more:
- safetyOutcome
- privacyConcern
- governanceRequirement
- languageRisk
- adoptionBlocker
- adoptionEnabler
- implementationQuestion

## Post-Session Debrief Checklist
- Top three trust blockers identified.
- Top three message improvements required.
- Policy clarifications needed.
- Product/UI implications captured.
- Follow-up questions for engineering/legal.
