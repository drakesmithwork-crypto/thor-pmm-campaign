# Advisory Board Research Plan

## Purpose
Establish a structured, small-scale advisory board process to gather direct input from students, parents, educators, and district stakeholders on trust, adoption, and communication requirements for Thor.

## Research Objectives
- Identify primary trust enablers and trust blockers by stakeholder group.
- Validate which safety outcomes are most meaningful to each audience.
- Surface privacy and governance expectations before campaign language is finalized.
- Test message framing options to reduce fear and improve comprehension.
- Generate actionable recommendations for PMM, policy, and product UI language.

## Participant Profile and Target Mix
Total recommended participants for first wave: 18-24

- Students (high school where permitted): 4-6
- Parents/guardians: 6-8
- Educators (teachers/counselors): 4-6
- Administrators/safety leads: 2-4
- Optional public safety liaison participants: 2

## Inclusion Guidance
- Include representation from varied school settings (urban/suburban/rural if possible).
- Include participants with different prior trust levels in technology and law enforcement.
- Include multilingual communication needs where feasible.

## Research Format
- 60-75 minute moderated sessions.
- Mix of small-group discussions and role-based breakouts.
- Optional pre-read: one-page neutral concept brief and guardrail summary.
- Optional post-session pulse survey (5-8 questions).

## Session Structure (Recommended)
1. Welcome, purpose, and consent framing (10 min).
2. Concept walkthrough in plain language (10 min).
3. Guided discussion on safety value and perceived risks (20 min).
4. Messaging and policy reaction exercise (15 min).
5. Priority ranking and closing reflections (10-15 min).

## Core Research Themes
- Perceived safety utility.
- Privacy boundaries and red lines.
- Activation legitimacy and human control expectations.
- Acceptable vs unacceptable use cases.
- Transparency requirements (who, what, when, why communication).
- Conditions required for adoption support.

## Capture and Documentation Approach
- Use a standardized note template per session.
- Capture direct quotes with anonymous participant labels.
- Tag findings by severity: blocker, concern, neutral, enabler.
- Map findings to PMM implications: messaging, policy, UI, launch sequencing.

## Risk and Ethics Considerations
- Avoid fear-inducing scenario framing during sessions.
- Provide clear non-surveillance positioning before reactions are gathered.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal data.
- Protect participant anonymity in synthesis outputs.

## Outputs
- Stakeholder sentiment map.
- Ranked trust concerns by audience.
- Message resonance analysis.
- Recommended policy language updates.
- Campaign implication summary.

## Timeline (2-3 Weeks)
- Week 1: Recruit participants, finalize script and materials.
- Week 2: Run sessions and pulse surveys.
- Week 3: Synthesize findings and produce recommendations.

## Decision Use
Insights from advisory board research should be required input for:
- Ethical positioning playbook refinements.
- Campaign message hierarchy and objection handling.
- Dashboard label/wording choices for trust-sensitive UI states.
- Community communication plan for launch readiness.
