# Mock Webpage Content Map

## Purpose
Translate approved PMM artifacts into a single structured webpage architecture that clearly communicates Thor's value, governance boundaries, and operational model to a mixed audience.

## Webpage Goal
Provide a trustworthy, executive-quality narrative experience that answers:
- What Thor is.
- Why schools would consider it.
- How privacy and governance are protected.
- How operations work during a critical incident.
- What stakeholders should do next.

## Primary Audiences and Content Priorities
- School boards/district leaders: value, governance, implementation readiness.
- Parents/educators: privacy boundaries, transparency, human control.
- Safety/public safety stakeholders: operational workflow and role clarity.

## Page Structure (Information Architecture)

### 1) Hero Section
- Headline: safety + trust-forward positioning.
- Subhead: incident-limited purpose with human governance.
- Primary CTA: "View How It Works".
- Secondary CTA: "Review Privacy Guardrails".

Content source: positioning brief + ethical playbook.

### 2) Problem and Opportunity
- Brief description of current emergency response visibility gap.
- Why first-minute decision quality matters.
- Non-fearful framing and context.

Content source: positioning brief.

### 3) What Thor Is (and Is Not)
- Product definition in one short paragraph.
- Two-column block:
  - "Thor helps with..."
  - "Thor is not used for..."

Content source: positioning brief + ethical playbook.

### 4) How It Works (Operational Flow)
- Step-based visual narrative from alert to closure.
- Human and system hand-offs highlighted.
- Link to ConOps detail anchor section.

Content source: ConOps deliverable.

### 5) School Security Dashboard Preview
- Mockup panel: normal state and active incident state.
- Labels for Sentry, Drone, Dock, alert queue, and dispatch status.
- Role-based access and audit cues.

Content source: dashboard mockup spec.

### 6) Privacy, Ethics, and Accountability
- Guardrail pillars (purpose, access, retention, oversight).
- Short policy commitments list.
- "Human decision required" callout.

Content source: ethical playbook.

### 7) Community FAQ
- Top concerns from parents/educators/students.
- Plain-language responses with policy-backed wording.

Content source: ethical playbook + advisory board insights.

### 8) Stakeholder-Specific Value Blocks
- Tabbed or stacked sections:
  - District leaders
  - Parents/educators
  - Public safety partners

Content source: positioning brief + campaign strategy.

### 9) Pilot and Readiness Framework
- Governance prerequisites.
- Training and implementation phases.
- Readiness checklist summary.

Content source: campaign strategy + engineering sync.

### 10) Closing CTA
- "Request Internal Briefing" (mock CTA).
- "Download Governance Summary" (mock CTA).
- "Review Incident Workflow" (anchor CTA).

## Suggested Page Components
- Sticky mini-nav to major sections.
- Callout cards for constraints and commitments.
- Timeline component for ConOps flow.
- Expand/collapse FAQ for readability.
- Auditability badge row (authorized access, human review, retention policy).

## Copy Deck Requirements
- Keep reading level plain-language friendly.
- Define all acronyms on first use (e.g., GSOC, ConOps).
- Avoid militarized or fear-triggering language.
- Separate validated capabilities from future-state concepts.

## Visual and UX Guidance
- Calm, neutral tone with minimal red/urgent accents outside active-incident examples.
- Use explanatory captions for all dashboard mock images.
- Ensure accessibility baseline (contrast, alt text, semantic heading hierarchy).

## Content Dependencies
Must be finalized before webpage build:
- Positioning brief.
- Ethical positioning playbook.
- ConOps flow content.
- Dashboard mockup annotation copy.
- Engineering claim validation checkpoint.

## Build-Ready Handoff Checklist
- Section-level copy approved.
- Visual placeholders mapped for each section.
- CTA language approved by PMM.
- FAQ reviewed for consistency with policy guardrails.
- Known limitations and disclaimers included.
